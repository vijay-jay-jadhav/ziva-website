Studio/About

About us

Painters, filmmakers, designers and writers who like working in the same room. Based in Pune, working in English, Marathi and Hindi.

Pull up a chair. The chai is on us.

How it started

One room where art, film and design feed each other

Ziva Studios was started in Pune by Pradnyesh Molak (Saaku), a filmmaker and traveller who wanted one place where a painter, an editor and a designer could sit at the same table.

Today the team brings decades of practice in painting and sculpture, cinematography and drone work, motion graphics, newsroom writing in English and Marathi, and data visualisation. A brand film, a mural and a logo can all start from the same idea.

Since 2018 we have also made our own films, interviews, travel stories and poetry for our channel, Zindagi Vasool.

What we believe

Three rules we keep at the table
01

We do not mass-produce

We curate, consult and craft. Every painting, film and identity is made for one client and one story.

02

Made to last

Archival oils on linen, museum glass, basalt stone. Our art is built to outlive the people who commission it.

03

One table, many hands

The painter sketches the storyboard, the filmmaker shoots the mural, the designer reads the data. Ideas cross rooms.
