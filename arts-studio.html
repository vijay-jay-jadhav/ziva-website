How a commission works

Five steps, from first look to final wall
  1. Step 1

    Awareness

    You see what we have made over the past decades, in halls, homes and public places.

  2. Step 2

    Interest

    We sit with you to understand the person, the place and the story the work should carry.

  3. Step 3

    Decision

    Sketches, materials and a clear quotation. Work starts only when you approve.

  4. Step 4

    Production

    The master artist and assistants build it by hand, in oil, clay or stone.

  5. Step 5

    Delivery & installation

    Packed with care, delivered and installed by our own team.
