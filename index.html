How a Ziva mural gets made

Every wall starts as a scribble on paper. Here is the route it takes before the scaffolding comes down.

  1. ScribbleA pencil idea, shaped with the client over chai and a few rounds of sketches.
  2. Square upThe sketch is gridded and scaled to the wall, square by square.
  3. Block inBase colours go on first, broad and flat, so the composition reads from across the street.
  4. Detail & sealFaces, lettering and fine work, then a protective coat so the wall lasts through the monsoon.

About the studio

Painters, filmmakers, designers and writers who like working in the same room

Ziva Studios was started in Pune by Pradnyesh Molak (Saaku), a filmmaker and traveller who wanted one place where art, film and design could feed each other.

The team brings decades of practice in painting and sculpture, cinematography and drone work, motion graphics, newsroom writing in English and Marathi, and data visualisation.
